Clinical Insight & Science

Why Donate Blood?

Donating blood is safe, simple, and has profound clinical benefits for recipients as well as regular voluntary donors.

workspace_premium Medically Certified Standards

Every 2 Seconds

Someone in our country requires an urgent blood transfusion due to surgery, trauma, cancer therapy, or chronic hematologic illness.

#CriticalDemand

Save Up To 3 Lives

A single whole blood donation can be split into three life-saving components: red blood cells, fresh frozen plasma, and platelets.

#ComponentTherapy

Cardiovascular Health

Regular blood donation helps reduce harmful iron overload, maintains healthy blood viscosity, and stimulates new red cell marrow production.

#DonorWellness

No Artificial Alternative

Blood cannot be manufactured or artificially synthesized in a laboratory. It can only come as a voluntary gift from one human to another.

#HumanGift

checklist Quick Donor Eligibility Baseline

  • checkAge: At least 17 years old (or 16 with parental consent)
  • checkWeight: Minimum of 110 lbs (50 kg) in good general health
  • checkInterval: 56 days between whole blood donations
  • checkScreening: Free mini-physical: hemoglobin, BP, pulse check

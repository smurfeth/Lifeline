Lifeline Staff Agreement & Terms

Clinical data protection and regulatory protocols

1. Authorized Clinical Access & Credential Verification

Access to the Lifeline Staff Portal is restricted strictly to licensed medical personnel, including registered physicians, nurses, medical technologists, and certified phlebotomists affiliated with accredited healthcare facilities.

2. Patient & Donor Confidentiality (RA 10173 & HIPAA)

In accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and international clinical confidentiality standards, all voluntary donor records, infectious disease screening results, deferral flags, and medical history must be maintained strictly confidential.

3. Phlebotomy & Clinical Screening Integrity

Staff members agree to record vital clinical baseline data (hemoglobin, blood pressure, pulse, deferral evaluations) accurately and truthfully. Any intentional falsification or negligence in screening constitutes immediate revocation of privileges and referral to PRC.

4. Blood Unit Traceability & Safety Standards

All blood collections, testing, component processing, and dispatch must adhere strictly to ISBT-128 labeling rules, chain of custody verification, and national blood transfusion guidelines.

5. Individual Non-Transferable Credentials

Staff login credentials and digital authorizations are strictly personal and non-transferable. You must never share passwords or access tokens with unauthorized individuals.

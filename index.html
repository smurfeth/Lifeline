CLINICAL INSIGHT & SCIENCE
Why Donate Blood?

Donating blood is safe, simple, and has profound clinical benefits for recipients as well
as regular voluntary donors.

Medically Certified Standards
Every 2 Seconds

Someone in our country requires an
urgent blood transfusion due to
surgery, trauma, cancer therapy, or
chronic hematologic illness.

#CriticalDemand

Save Up To 3 Lives

A single whole blood donation can
be split into three life-saving
components: red blood cells, fresh
frozen plasma, and platelets.

#ComponentTherapy
Cardiovascular Health

Regular blood donation helps reduce
harmful iron overload, maintains
healthy blood viscosity, and
stimulates new red cell marrow
production.

#DonorWellness
No Artificial Alternative

Blood cannot be manufactured or
artificially synthesized in a
laboratory. It can only come as a
voluntary gift from one human to
another.

#HumanGift
Quick Donor Eligibility Baseline

Age: At least 17 years old (or 16 with
parental consent)

Weight: Minimum of 110 lbs (50 kg) in
good general health

Interval: 56 days between whole
blood donations

Screening: Free mini-physical:
hemoglobin, BP, pulse check
